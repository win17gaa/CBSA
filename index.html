How to Use & Workflow

1. Log Daily (Once Only): One entry allowed per calendar day. Keep it honest and lock it in.

2. Export Text Report: At the end of the week, click "Export Text Report" to download your rolling log complete with automated trends.

3. The Second Opinion: Paste your exported text file into an LLM for pattern checks.

Note on Storage: Data lives strictly in this browser on this device. If you switch computers or clear your browser data without exporting first, your history will be lost.

What's CBSA, Anyway?

Think of CBSA as a quiet gauge under the hood of your day. You don't have to think about complex metrics — it's built to track something simpler: how are you actually doing, over time, not just today?

Every day, you jot a few honest lines — how the day felt, whether work went the way you planned, where your head's been living. That's it. No forms, no scoring yourself.

A quick, honest note: CBSA is a personal reflection tool. It's not a doctor, therapist, or crisis service. If you're genuinely struggling, please reach out to a real person.
