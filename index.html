How to Use This Tool

1. Log Once a Day: Save one entry per calendar day reflecting how your day actually went.

2. Export Report: At week's end, hit "Export Text Report" to download your rolling log file.

3. AI Audit: Drop that text file into any AI chat for a pattern check over time.

Data Storage: Everything stays securely right inside your browser.

Core Practice: Pacing & Latches

Watch Your Pace: If your physical or internal pace runs faster than normal, briefly slow down on purpose. Feel the drop, then keep going. Repetition retrains your baseline.

Log Thought Latches: Let background thoughts drift by, but notice what you actually latch onto. Don't fight it—just log it as clean data.
